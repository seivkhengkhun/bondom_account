This policy lists every cookie and browser storage item the site uses. The list is short because we run no advertising and no third-party analytics.

1. What we set

NameTypePurposeLifetime
bondom_session Cookie — strictly necessary Keeps you signed in after Telegram authentication. Signed with HMAC-SHA256, HTTP-only, Secure in production, SameSite=Lax. Contains your Telegram ID, username and an expiry — no password, no payment data. 30 days
theme localStorage — preference Remembers whether you chose the light or dark appearance. Never sent to the server. Until you clear it

2. What we do not set

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel).
  • No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting.

3. Third-party content

The sign-in button is the official Telegram Login Widget, loaded from telegram.org. Loading it means Telegram can see that your browser requested it, subject to Telegram's own privacy policy. It only appears on pages where you are not already signed in.

4. Consent

The session cookie is strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for — staying signed in — so it does not require consent under the usual cookie rules. The theme preference is stored locally on your own device and is never transmitted. Because we set no tracking cookies, there is no consent banner to click through.

5. Controlling cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Removing bondom_session signs you out; removing theme returns the site to following your system appearance. Neither affects your account, balance, or purchase history.

6. Changes

If we ever add a cookie, this page is updated before it ships.
