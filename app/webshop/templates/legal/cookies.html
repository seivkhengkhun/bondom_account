This policy lists every cookie and browser storage item the site uses. The list is short because we run no advertising and no third-party analytics.

1. What we set

NameTypePurposeLifetime
bondom_session Cookie — strictly necessary Keeps you signed in after Telegram authentication. Signed with HMAC-SHA256, HTTP-only, Secure in production, SameSite=Lax. Contains your Telegram ID, username and an expiry — no password, no payment data. 30 days

That is the only item. The site has a single dark appearance and stores no display preferences, and it does not use localStorage, sessionStorage or other browser storage.

2. What we do not set

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel).
  • No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting.

3. Third-party content

The sign-in button is the official Telegram Login Widget, loaded from telegram.org. Loading it means Telegram can see that your browser requested it, subject to Telegram's own privacy policy. It is only loaded while you are signed out: automatically on pages that show a sign-in panel, and on other pages only when you open the sign-in window.

Fonts, icons and animated emoji are served from our own domain. Animated emoji are fetched from Telegram by our server, not by your browser.

4. Consent

The session cookie is strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for — staying signed in — so it does not require consent under the usual cookie rules. Because we set no tracking cookies and store nothing else in your browser, there is no consent banner to click through.

5. Controlling cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser settings. Removing bondom_session signs you out; it does not affect your account, balance, or purchase history.

Earlier versions of the site saved a theme item in localStorage to remember a light or dark choice. The site no longer reads or writes it. If your browser still has it, you can clear it safely.

6. Changes

If we ever add a cookie, this page is updated before it ships.
